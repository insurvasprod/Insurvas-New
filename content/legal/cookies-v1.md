This notice explains the cookies and similar browser storage Insurvas uses. It forms part of our
Privacy Policy.

## 1. In short

We use **only strictly necessary cookies**, to keep you signed in and to complete two-step
verification. We do **not** use analytics, advertising, social-media or other tracking cookies, and
we do not let third parties set cookies through our site. Because these cookies are needed for the
service to work, there is no setting to turn them off; blocking them in your browser will stop you
from signing in.

## 2. Cookies we set

| Cookie | Purpose | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `insurvas_tenant_session` | Keeps an agency user signed in | Up to 12 hours |
| `insurvas_tenant_2fa` | Holds the sign-in while an agency user completes two-step verification | 5 minutes |
| `insurvas_partner_session` | Keeps a lead-vendor partner signed in to the partner portal | Until the browser closes, or up to 12 hours if "remember me" is chosen |
| `insurvas_admin_session` | Keeps Insurvas staff signed in to the staff console | Until the browser closes (the session itself expires after 12 hours) |
| `insurvas_admin_pending_2fa` | Holds a staff sign-in while two-step verification is completed | 5 minutes |

All of these are first-party, are not readable by page scripts (HttpOnly), are sent only over
encrypted connections in production (Secure), and are not sent with requests from other sites
(SameSite=Lax).

## 3. Browser storage

We also use your browser's local storage and session storage. This data stays on your device; it
is not sent to us automatically and is not used to track you. It holds:

- **Preferences** (local storage): light or dark theme, notification sound and snooze choices,
  saved table views, whether a setup checklist is hidden, and your recent searches inside the
  product.
- **Work in progress** (session storage, cleared when the tab closes): for example whether the
  website intro has already played, and, while you import a lead file, a copy of that file so the
  import can be reviewed before it is saved. Lead files can contain personal data; close the tab
  when you finish an import on a shared computer.

You can clear this storage at any time in your browser settings; the product will fall back to
its defaults.

## 4. Payment pages

When you check out, you leave our site for our payment provider's hosted page. That page is
governed by the provider's own cookie and privacy notices.

## 5. Changes

If we add any cookie that is not strictly necessary — for example for analytics — we will update
this notice first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent before setting it.
