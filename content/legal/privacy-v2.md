# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("**Insurvas**", "**we**", "**us**" or
"**our**") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information in connection with our
website and the Insurvas service, and the choices and rights available to you.

**The key points, in brief.** This summary is for convenience; the full policy below controls.

- Insurvas is software for insurance agencies. For information about our customers and their
  users, we decide how it is used, and this policy applies.
- For information that agencies store about their own clients and prospects, we act only on the
  agency's behalf and on its instructions. The agency's privacy notice applies to that information.
- We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not
  use it to train artificial-intelligence models.
- We use only the cookies needed to run the service, and no advertising or analytics cookies.
- The most sensitive fields, such as Social Security and bank account numbers, are encrypted with
  a key specific to each agency, and revealing them requires two-step verification and is logged.

## 1. Scope and our role

Insurvas is a business service used by insurance agencies, call centres, marketing organisations
and independent agents (each an "**agency**"). Our role depends on whose information it is.

- **Information about our customers and their users.** This includes agency owners and staff,
  lead vendors and other partners who use our partner portal, and visitors to our website. We
  decide why and how this information is used, and this policy describes our practices.
- **Information agencies store about their consumers.** This includes the leads, prospects,
  applicants, insureds and beneficiaries an agency works with ("**consumers**"). We process this
  information as a service provider and processor to the agency, only to provide the service and
  as the agency instructs, under our agreement with the agency, including our Data Processing
  Agreement. The agency decides what it collects and how it uses it, and the agency's own privacy
  notice governs it. If you are a consumer, please direct questions and requests to the agency you
  dealt with (see section 9).

## 2. Information we collect about customers and users

- **Account information:** name, work email address, mobile phone number, role and permissions,
  profile photo if you add one, and your password, which we store only as a one-way hash.
- **Agency information:** agency name and trading name, address, National Producer Number, licence
  numbers and licensed states, carrier appointments and writing numbers, logo, and tax
  identification number. The tax identification number is stored encrypted and displayed only by
  its last four digits.
- **Security information:** two-step verification settings and hashed recovery codes; sign-in
  records, including IP address, browser and device information, time, and whether the attempt
  succeeded; and active sessions. We record failed sign-in attempts because this is how account
  takeover is detected.
- **Legal acceptance records:** which version of which document you accepted, when, and from which
  IP address and browser.
- **Billing information:** plan, billing period, subscription status, invoices, and the customer
  and membership references our payment provider gives us. Your payment card is entered on our
  payment provider's hosted page, and we do not receive or store the full card number.
- **Calendar information (optional):** if you connect a Google or Microsoft calendar, the
  calendar account's email address, an access token that we store encrypted, and your free and
  busy time. We request free/busy access only, and we do not read event titles, attendees or
  email.
- **Partner information:** for lead vendors and other partners, name, email, organisation, and
  the leads and messages the partner submits to an agency.
- **Usage and audit information:** actions taken in the service, such as who changed a record and
  when, kept in audit logs; and usage counts used to apply plan limits and metered billing.
- **Communications:** messages you send us, feedback you submit, and records of the emails we send
  you.

## 3. Information processed on behalf of agencies

Depending on the features an agency uses, the information it stores in Insurvas about its
consumers may include:

- **Contact and lead information:** name, phone numbers, email address, postal address, date of
  birth, the lead's source, vendor and cost, call outcomes and dispositions, notes, callbacks and
  appointments.
- **Consent and compliance records:** consent certificates and their identifiers (for example
  TrustedForm certificates or Jornaya LeadiD tokens supplied with a lead), the IP address and web
  page recorded with the consent, do-not-call and suppression-list entries, and the results of
  pre-dial screening checks.
- **Insurance application information:** Social Security number; date of birth; answers to health
  questions; medications, doses and the conditions they treat; income; beneficiaries, who may
  include minor children; and premium payment details, which may include bank routing and account
  numbers or payment card numbers.
- **Policy and commission information:** carrier statements and files, policy numbers and status,
  premiums, and commission records.
- **Uploaded files:** carrier statements, application confirmations, compliance files and other
  attachments, including carrier statements that an agency arranges to have emailed to its
  Insurvas address.

Agencies are responsible for having a lawful basis, and for giving any notices and obtaining any
consents required, for the consumer information they put into Insurvas, including under
insurance, telemarketing and privacy laws that apply to them.

## 4. How we use information

We use information about our customers and their users to:

- provide, operate and maintain the service, including signing you in, applying roles,
  permissions and plan limits, and carrying out the actions you request;
- secure the service, including detecting and preventing fraud, misuse and unauthorised access;
- process payments, through our payment provider, for your plan, add-ons and metered usage;
- communicate with you about your account, such as email verification, trial and billing notices,
  security alerts, and changes to our terms and policies, and contact you by phone about your
  account where you have given us a number;
- provide support, and understand how the service is used so that we can improve it; and
- comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

We use consumer information only to provide the service to the agency that submitted it, on that
agency's instructions, and as required by law.

**What we do not do.** We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context
behavioural advertising. We do not use agency or consumer information to train
artificial-intelligence models, and we do not send personal information to any
artificial-intelligence provider.

**Dictation.** Where a user dictates a note, speech is converted to text either by the user's
browser or by a speech model that runs entirely on the user's device. With the browser option,
the audio is processed by the browser's provider under that provider's terms. With the on-device
option, the audio never leaves the user's computer. We do not receive or store audio.

## 5. How we disclose information

We disclose personal information only as described below.

- **Service providers.** We use carefully selected providers to host and run the service,
  process payments, send and receive email, and provide optional features such as calendar
  connection, consent-certificate retention and number screening. They may process personal
  information only on our instructions and under written obligations of confidentiality and
  security. Our current list of these providers, with what each does and where it processes data,
  is published at [insurvas.com/legal/subprocessors].
- **Payment provider.** Subscription payments are handled by Whop, Inc., which receives the
  information you provide at checkout and processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **At an agency's direction.** We share information where an agency instructs us to, for example
  when it connects a webhook, posts or exports data to its own systems, uses our API, invites a
  partner, or uses our browser extension to fill a carrier's application form.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that it is
  required by law, subpoena or court order, or necessary to protect the rights, safety or property
  of Insurvas, our customers, consumers or others.
- **Business transfers.** If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets,
  information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only strictly necessary cookies, which keep you signed in and protect sign-in and two-step
verification, and your browser's local storage for preferences such as your theme and recent
searches. We do not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our Cookie Notice
at [insurvas.com/legal/cookies] has more detail.

## 7. How long we keep information

- **Active accounts.** We keep customer and user information for as long as the agency's account
  is active.
- **After cancellation.** When an agency cancels, its workspace remains available in read-only mode
  for [90] days so that it can export its data, after which it can no longer be accessed. An
  agency may ask us to delete its remaining data at any time, and we will delete or de-identify it
  within [30] days, except as described below.
- **Consumer information.** Agencies control how long consumer information is kept. The service
  lets agencies set retention rules and delete a consumer's records on request. Numbers on an
  internal do-not-call list are kept so that the person is not contacted again.
- **Records we must keep.** We keep certain records for longer where needed for legal, tax,
  accounting, security or dispute-resolution purposes, such as invoices and payment records,
  sign-in and audit logs, and legal acceptance records.

## 8. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information
appropriately for its sensitivity. They include:

- separation of each agency's data at the database level, so that one agency cannot access
  another's records;
- field-level encryption, with a key specific to each agency, of Social Security numbers, bank
  account and routing numbers, and payment card numbers entered on applications, as well as tax
  identification numbers, calendar tokens and two-step verification secrets;
- two-step verification, which is required before sensitive fields can be revealed, and a log of
  every such reveal;
- role-based access controls and audit logs of changes to records;
- passwords stored only as one-way hashes; and
- encryption of data in transit and at rest.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal information, we will notify
affected customers and individuals, and regulators where required, as the law requires.

## 9. Your privacy rights

**Agency users and website visitors.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know what personal information we have collected about you and how we use and disclose it;
- access and obtain a copy of your personal information;
- correct inaccurate personal information;
- delete your personal information; and
- opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for targeted advertising, or certain
  profiling. We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or engage in
  such profiling.

These rights may be available to residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws, such as
California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon, subject to the exemptions
those laws provide. To make a request, email info@insurvas.com. We will verify your identity before
acting on a request and respond within the time required by applicable law. Where the law allows,
you may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf. If we decline your request, you
may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to your appeal in writing within the
time required by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

**Consumers of an agency.** If an agency stores your information in Insurvas, that agency controls
it and is responsible for responding to your requests. Please contact the agency directly. If you
contact us, we will forward your request to the agency and assist it in responding.
