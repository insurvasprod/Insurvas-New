3 · Could a salesperson set this up? Not today.

Walking it as demo.ledger took ten separate stops, each with its own words:
  1. Settings › Carriers: tick carriers, type a "contract level" (what's a level?)
  2. States: tick states, then tick carriers per state
  3. Statements: upload, pick carrier and period, check column mapping, preview, import
  4. Every line "No match": the book was empty, nothing said why
  5. Policies: a different import with its own template
  6. Back to the statement: Re-Match, then Accept All
  7. Rate card: per carrier, product → rate → "for level" → from-date
  8. Advance rules: months, %, clawback months, prorated vs full
  9. Pay issues: Check Now
  10. Only then: "$882 owed to you"
Steps 5, 7 and 8 need insurance-back-office words ("level", "advance %", "clawback") and none of it is required to see the money a statement already holds. Most of it can be read off the statement itself: the carrier from the file, policies from the policy numbers and names, the rate from the Rate column, the advance from "paid ÷ premium" (810 on 1,080 = 75%).
