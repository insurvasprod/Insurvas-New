The 12 guides: content to review

Same words in every template. Tell me what's wrong or missing; the order follows a lead becoming money

1 · Leads

Owner · manager · Lead workspace, Lead flow, Vendors & campaigns, Duplicate check, Lead recycling
How it works
  1. Each lead buy is a campaign under its vendor.
  2. You import the vendor's file into that campaign.
  3. Insurvas checks every lead: do-not-call, known litigators, duplicates, before anyone can dial it.
  4. Routing hands leads to agents who are licensed and appointed in the lead's state.
  5. Leads nobody reached go to recycling on a schedule.
Why we built it
  • Spreadsheets lose where a lead came from, so nobody knows which vendor really sells.
  • One TCPA claim from a bad list costs more than the list.
What it does for you
  • Owner: cost per sale for every vendor and campaign.
  • Producer: only clean leads you can legally call, in your states.
First clicks: Vendors & campaigns › New campaign · Lead flow › Import · Route

2 · Today: dialer, inbound, callbacks, calendar

Producer · setter · Dialer, Inbound, Callbacks, Calendar, Appointments & setters
How it works
  1. The dialer serves your next lead: assigned to you, inside its calling hours, best first.
  2. Inbound transfers wait in a queue. Claim one before its time runs out, or it escalates.
  3. A call runs check, verify, underwriting, finish on one screen.
  4. Every call ends with an outcome. The outcome moves the lead, or books a callback.
Why we built it
  • Agents waste the first hour of every day deciding who to call.
  • Calling outside the hours, or forgetting a callback, loses the sale or breaks the law.
What it does for you
  • Producer: one button, "Serve next lead", all day.
  • Manager: no transfer sits unclaimed. You see who's on a call.
First clicks: Dialer › Serve next lead · record the outcome · Callbacks

3 · Sales: quotes and applications

Producer · reviewer · Applications, Quotes, Pending cases, Draft dates, Application review, Daily deal flow
How it works
  1. Quote from the call: products you sell, carriers you're appointed with.
  2. Fill the application once: health questions, beneficiary, payment.
  3. Submit, and the case moves through underwriting in Pending cases.
  4. Draft dates show when each first premium comes out.
Why we built it
  • Each carrier portal asks the same things differently, and cases get lost between them.
  • A missed draft date is a policy that never goes in force.
What it does for you
  • Producer: quote to submitted without leaving the call.
  • Owner: daily deal flow: what was written today, by whom.
First clicks: a lead › Start application · Pending cases · Daily deal flow

4 · Book of business

Owner · bookkeeper · Carrier files, Policies, Commission ledger
How it works
  1. Upload each carrier's policy report.
  2. Each policy gets a status: issued, in force, lapsed, charged back.
  3. Its premium, commission and history sit on one page.
  4. The ledger lists every dollar per policy.
Why we built it
  • Your book is spread across carrier portals. Nobody has the whole picture.
  • Retention and commission checks need one true list of policies.
What it does for you
  • Owner: the agency's real book, and what it's worth.
  • Producer: your own policies and their status.
First clicks: Carrier files › Upload · Policies · open one policy

5 · Carrier sync

Owner · bookkeeper · Needs you, Rate card, Carrier terms, Card moves, Agent codes, Files in, Lapse risk
How it works
  1. Tell Insurvas each carrier's terms once: rates, advance, chargeback window.
  2. Map each agent code to a person.
  3. Every file that comes in then reads itself.
  4. Needs you lists only what it couldn't place.
Why we built it
  • Every carrier's file looks different. Without a setup, every month is manual.
  • An unknown agent code means unpaid commission.
What it does for you
  • Owner/bookkeeper: set up once, then a few minutes a month on Needs you.
First clicks: Needs you · Agent codes › map one · Carrier terms

6 · Commissions

Owner · bookkeeper · producer · Statements, Pay issues, Who gets the credit, Producer pay, Team payouts, Splits, Partner payouts, My pay
How it works
  1. Upload each carrier statement.
  2. Every line is matched to a policy.
  3. Missing, short-paid or wrong lines become pay issues you can claim.
  4. Credit goes to the writing agent, splits and overrides.
  5. Pay producers, the team and partners from what came in.
Why we built it
  • Carriers short-pay and charge back wrongly. It goes unnoticed without a line-by-line check.
What it does for you
  • Owner: money owed comes back.
  • Producer: My pay shows exactly what you earned and why.
First clicks: Statements › Import · Pay issues · My pay

7 · Retention

Retention specialist · owner · Cases, Waiting on the client, Win-back, Lapse risk, Retention settings
How it works
  1. Lapse risk flags policies likely to lapse: missed draft, payment problem, carrier notice.
  2. Flagged policies become cases in a save queue.
  3. Work each case: call, fix the payment, re-date the draft.
  4. Lost ones go to win-back.
Why we built it
  • A lapse in year one means the carrier takes the advance back.
  • Saving a policy is far cheaper than selling a new one.
What it does for you
  • Owner: fewer chargebacks and better persistency, so a higher contract level.
  • Specialist: one queue, best saves first.
First clicks: Cases › open the first · log the outcome

8 · Accounting

Bookkeeper · owner · To do, Vendor bills, Vendor returns, Expenses, Producer splits, Bank deposits, Profit & loss, Tax summaries
How it works
  1. Vendor bills are checked against the leads you actually got.
  2. Bank deposits are matched to carrier statements.
  3. Expenses and producer splits go to the right month.
  4. Profit & loss and tax summaries build themselves.
Why we built it
  • Agencies overpay vendors and don't know their real profit per month.
  • A separate bookkeeping tool never knows about leads or policies.
What it does for you
  • Bookkeeper: one To do list each month.
  • Owner: real profit, by month and by vendor.
First clicks: To do · Bank deposits › match · Profit & loss

9 · Partners

Owner · manager · Publishers, Partner chat, partner portal
How it works
  1. Add each publisher who sends you leads or live transfers.
  2. They get their own portal and a chat with you.
  3. Every lead and transfer they send is tracked to a result.
Why we built it
  • Publisher deals live in texts and email, and quality is guessed.
What it does for you
  • Owner: pay partners for what converts, from their quality report.
First clicks: Publishers › Add publisher · Partner chat

10 · Compliance

Owner · compliance · Overview, TCPA / DNC, Consent locker, Complaints, Deletion requests, Evidence report, Carrier appointments
How it works
  1. Every lead's consent is kept in the consent locker.
  2. Do-not-call and litigator checks run on import and before dialing.
  3. Complaints and deletion requests are logged with deadlines.
  4. An evidence report is one click when a claim arrives.
Why we built it
  • A single TCPA claim can cost more than a year's profit.
  • The proof must exist before the claim, not after.
What it does for you
  • Owner: proof ready, deadlines met.
  • Producer: you can't dial someone you shouldn't.
First clicks: Overview · Consent locker › search a number · Evidence report

11 · Reports

Owner · manager · Sales performance, Team activity, Queue scoring, Book scorecards, True CPA, Partner quality, Retention, Producer scorecards, Hierarchy & overrides
How it works
  1. Every report reads the same data: leads, calls, applications, policies, money.
  2. Pick a period. Click any number for the records behind it.
Why we built it
  • The real questions: which vendor, which producer, which carrier makes money. They need lead-to-money data in one place.
What it does for you
  • Owner: true cost per sale before you buy more leads.
  • Manager: who needs coaching, from activity and placement.
First clicks: True CPA · Producer scorecards · Sales performance

12 · Home and dashboards

Everyone · Home, Agency dashboard, Compare
How it works
  1. Home answers "what do I do today": Needs you, Today, My numbers, Shortcuts. Each role gets its own.
  2. The agency dashboard answers "is the agency healthy": five figures, business health, producers.
  3. Compare puts two periods or two people side by side.
Why we built it
  • Every number should link to the records behind it, so nobody exports to a spreadsheet.
What it does for you
  • Producer: start every day from Home.
  • Owner: the agency on one screen each week.
First clicks: Home › Needs you · Agency › Compare
