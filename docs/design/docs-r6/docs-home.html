Docs is where a new agent or a new agency owner learns two things: how to get into insurance, and how to put Insurvas to work. Today it is a list of chapters, and Insurvas itself (each module, what it does, why it's there) has no pages at all: only the 18 setup steps do.

1 · Three progress numbers that disagreeThe menu says "1 of 17 read", the chapter says "15/18", a page says "1 of 7 done". "Read", "done in Insurvas" and "ticked by you" are different things with the same bar.
2 · No "using Insurvas" chapterSetup tells you which button to click, never what a module is for. Nobody explains why Carrier sync, Pay issues or the Consent locker exist, or when you'd open them.
3 · No next step on the home pageThe home page doesn't say where you are or what to do next. The only "next" lives in Get set up.
4 · Chapters read like a bookSix equal cards. A new agent can't tell that one chapter is three months of their life and another is a one-hour setup.
