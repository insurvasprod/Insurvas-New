Light theme: three directions

Dark already works because its greys carry the brand's olive and its cards were designed. Light was never designed: it kept the plain card, its greys are a cool blue-grey that fights the green brand, and one heavy grey (#e4e8ee) paints tiles, table heads, chips and hovers everywhere. Each direction below is a complete token set. The screens are the same; only the tokens change, so what you pick here is exactly what gets built.

1. Colours that matchLight greys move from blue (~220°) to the brand's own green or a warm stone, like dark already does.
2. Cards you can seeToday a see-through card with no shadow sits on a page 1.06:1 away. Light cards become solid, with a tinted edge and a soft shadow.
3. Less greyTiles, rows and hovers get a faint wash instead of a grey slab. The grey band is kept for table heads only.
4. Nothing hiddenRow lines, muted text and hovers get real contrast. Figures are in ink; colour lives in one chip.
Keys 1–5 switch. Every screen below follows the switch.

A · Sage (recommended)

The greys take the brand's green, the same family as dark's olive and the landing page. Cards are solid white with a green-tinted edge and a soft olive shadow; tiles are a faint wash. Closest to dark, one palette for the whole product.

B · Warm paper

Ivory page and warm stone greys. Calm and editorial, pairs well with the olive, but it is a new feel not shared with dark or the landing page.

C · White + olive lines

A near-white page; structure comes from green-tinted lines and header bands instead of fills or shadows. The lightest and crispest, closest to a spreadsheet look.
