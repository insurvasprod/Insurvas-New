Dashboards by role, partner portals by type, and what's left for premium

Three audits from the code and Notion (Nov 18): every role's dashboard and access, the partner portal against the Notion partner spec (LA-1.1/1.2/1.8/1.9/1.17/1.19/7.4), and an app-wide premium review. The findings come first, then one compact dashboard per role in the agency-dashboard style (no page scroll, numbers with change badges, one big chart, every figure a link), then one portal per partner type. Marked New = data or a feature that doesn't exist yet; Fix = a bug found in the audit.

What the audits found

Ranked inside each column
Role dashboards (app)
  1. Money leaks: managers see $/mo on "My recent business" and +$/yr in the agency's Recent activity; retention reps get a whole money view ("$ saved", $/mo column) though their role has no money permission. Fix
  2. The dashboard ignores agency narrowing: setters and assistants get an empty Compliance tab; the assistant's "Import a list" leads to "owners only".
  3. "My numbers" is computed for every role (setter booked/shown/show rate, producer close-out, bookkeeper money strip) but never drawn.
  4. One layout for all roles: dead cards ("Calls show here once…") for assistant and retention, an empty right column for retention, and the Home page scrolls.
  5. Daily pages never surfaced: producer My pay/ledger, bookkeeper Statements/Payouts, retention Scorecard, manager Producers/Hierarchy.
  6. No stored goals: producer goal + Set goal, setter weekly target, retention monthly target. New
Partner portal
  1. The portal doesn't know the partner's type, so publishers, marketing partners and affiliates get the identical call-centre portal.
  2. Affiliates can't see their own tracked link, campaign or clicks; links live only on the agency side.
  3. Everyone submits the full six-section application; the affiliate short form exists only on the public link page.
  4. The pipeline picker lists every pipeline the agency has, so a marketing partner sees the publisher and affiliate pipeline names.
  5. Deal flow, Team performance and Team access are shown to a one-person affiliate; marketing has no campaign view and no "what is owed".
  6. The board shows 4 shared lanes, not the type's own stages (an owner decision on Oct 30 that reversed LA-1.17; asks for a fresh decision).
What's left for "premium"
  1. Production jobs are failing (16 of 19: the app refuses the database's scheduled calls) → no digests, reminders or licence warnings reach anyone. Config, small.
  2. Production config: payments still on sandbox IDs, Google sign-in has no redirect address, staff console served on customer hosts.
  3. Built features waiting on database pastes: about 32 screens can say "needs a database update".
  4. Security items open from the Nov review (unmasked SSN/bank until its paste lands, 2FA brute force, security headers, public live channels…).
  5. No SMS or push for money-losing events (draft failed, callback due), as the spec promises.
  6. AI assistant is a placeholder until a provider with health-data terms is chosen.
  7. No built-in calling (click-to-call only), no call recording.
  8. Saved views only per browser; no scheduled emailed reports; @mentions not working; no QuickBooks/Xero; owner can't require 2FA for the team.
