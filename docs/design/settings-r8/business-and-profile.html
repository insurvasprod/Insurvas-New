Settings › Business identity & My profile

Business identity is who the agency is on paper: names and numbers carriers check, the address and logo printed on every letter and statement, and how partners reach you. My profile is the same for one person: your details, how you sign in, and which alerts reach you. Today both are split into tabs, so a field the documents need sits on a different tab from the list that says it is missing, and each page has more than one way to save. Your ask: each one becomes a single page whose form scrolls inside one card, with the title pinned and one save bar.

1 · Four tabs for one formBusiness identity spreads 17 fields over Business, Contact & address, Documents & logo and Partners & owner. The documents list says "needs a business email", but the email box is two tabs away.
2 · Three ways to saveThe save bar for most fields, a separate Save button for partner support, and the profile photo that saves the moment you pick it. You can't tell what is kept.
3 · Internals on showPartners & owner prints the raw workspace ID. Owners have no use for it; support asks for it once a year.
4 · Security is hidden behind a tabTwo-step verification being off is only seen if you open Sign-in & security. History is tabs inside a tab (Everything / Profile / Sign-in).
5 · Two of everything, unexplainedBusiness time zone and your time zone; business phone, your phone and partner support phone. Each needs one plain line saying which is which.
6 · Problems arrive as notices"Saving needs a database update" and save errors draw cards above the form and push it down. They belong on the status line.

Pick one

Nothing is built until you choose · the edit surfaces and states come with every option
A · Index + scrolling form (recommended)One card; a sticky section index on the left with a done mark per section; the form scrolls on the right. It's the frame already approved on the foundation board.
  • 4 tabs become 4 sections on one page, for both pages
  • Documents-ready becomes a slim progress line that links straight to the missing field, and the field is marked
  • Partner support and the photo join the one save bar; email, password and two-step keep their own confirm
  • History is a sheet, not tabs in a tab
  • Workspace ID only behind "Show technical details" + Copy
B · Form + live previewSame single scrolling form, with a "How you appear" card beside it (letterhead, payout statement, client portal; for My profile, client card and email signature).
  • You see the letter change as you type
  • Jump chips instead of a side index
  • Narrower form; the preview costs a third of the width on every visit
C · Sections that foldEach section is a row with a one-line summary and a completeness chip; open one or all.
  • Shortest first view: the whole agency in four lines
  • Problems show on the closed row ("1 to add", "Two-step off")
  • More clicks to edit, and an open section can hide what is unsaved in another
