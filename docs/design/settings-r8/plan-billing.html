Settings › Plan & billing

Plan & billing tells the owner what they pay, how close they are to the plan's limits, and lets them change plan, update the card, pay an invoice, or pause or cancel. Today it is three tabs (Overview, Change plan, Invoices & payment). The most important facts, a failed payment, a paused or cancelling workspace, arrive as notice cards and in the bell, and cancelling expands inline under the plan card.

1 · The state of the account is a noticeDunning, read-only, paused, cancelling and cancelled draw cards above the plan, and also go to the bell. They belong on the status line, with the one action that fixes them.
2 · Limits are found lateMeters sit under the plan card on Overview; the 80% and 95% warnings are a hover hint. Text messages at 96% look the same as seats at 68%.
3 · Changing plan is its own tabPlan cards, add-ons and the impact card stack down a separate tab; the "what you lose" and "you're over its limits" warnings are notices again.
4 · Cancel unfolds inside the pageReason, export links and a one-time "pause instead?" open under the plan card and push everything down. A calm, stepped sheet would read better and keep pause first.
5 · Money in two placesNext charge sits on Invoices & payment, the price on Overview, add-ons on Change plan. One look should answer "what will I pay on Dec 3, and why".
6 · Gold must not mean "warning"Limits use the warning tone. Gold stays for one feature button (Upgrade), people and new counts.

Pick one

Nothing is built until you choose · sheets, dialogs and states come with every option
A · Plan hero + sheets (recommended)One page, no tabs: the plan and its meters on top, invoices and payment side by side below. Everything you edit opens as a sheet.
  • Three tabs become one page; "what do I pay on Dec 3, and why" is answered in one look
  • Meters turn warning-toned at 80% and 95% with the reset date; one gold Upgrade
  • Compare plans and the change impact move to right sheets; confirm in a dialog with dollars
  • Pause and cancel become a calm, stepped sheet with pause first, offered once
  • Dunning, paused, cancelling and cancelled ride the status line with their one fix, not notices or the bell
B · Usage firstBig meters with a 7-day trend and a "you'll reach it on…" forecast; the plan is a side card. Invoices & payment is a second header tab.
  • Best for agencies that run close to limits
  • Forecast per meter is new (needs daily usage)
  • Price and invoices are one click further away
C · One statementA single inner-scroll card read top to bottom: plan, usage, invoices, payment, pause or cancel, with a section index.
  • Calmest, most like a bank statement
  • Cancel last, in plain words
  • Problems lower down need the status line and index marks to be seen
