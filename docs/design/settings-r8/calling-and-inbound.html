Settings › Calling windows · Inbound settings

Two pages that decide when the dialer may ring a customer and how long a live transfer may wait before someone is told. Today both are forms of typed values: hours as two time boxes, a ladder of durations typed as text like "45 seconds", and campaign narrowing hidden inside dialogs. The owner cannot see the week or the wait. This wave-3 board shows one recommended design for each page.

1 · You can't see the weekAgency hours are two time boxes and three switches. Nothing shows that Saturday is shorter, that Sunday is off, or that Florida stops an hour before you do.
2 · State rules sit apart from your hours"State rules in force" is a read-only list under the form, so the owner has to work out in their head which state cuts which evening.
3 · Campaign narrowing is buriedA paged table where each row opens a centred dialog with two more time boxes; the reason ("Vendor consent language is daytime-only") is easy to miss and the result is never drawn.
4 · Durations typed as free textWarn, Escalate, Partner notice and Expire are text fields: "45 seconds", "1.5 min", "90s". A typo is only caught on save, and the order of the rungs is never shown.
5 · One save bar, two hidden savesInbound saves to two APIs behind one bar; if one half fails the page says "saved" in the bell and the other half is lost.
6 · Switches with no grouping"Who hears about it", buffer agents and "Who gets the next transfer" are one column of switches with long labels. It reads like a policy document, not a set-up screen.

What changes vs today

both pages keep every setting they have; only how they are shown and saved changes

Calling windows

  • Two time boxes become a week grid: drag a day's edges in 15-minute steps; each day can differ (today one window for all days) needs a migration
  • "State rules in force" moves beside the grid; pick a state and its cut-offs are drawn on the week
  • No Sundays and No federal holidays stay as switches in the card header; the holiday list opens as a sheet with each date
  • Per-campaign narrowing becomes "Campaigns that call less": rows open a right sheet with a mini week, a reason and what happens to waiting leads
  • The legal-review label moves into the status line
  • One save bar for agency hours; a campaign sheet saves itself, like every record

Inbound settings

  • Warn / Escalate / Partner notice / Expire become a timeline with draggable rungs; − / + and chips in 15-second steps; no typed durations
  • Rungs can't cross; the limits are stated where you edit
  • A plain-English sentence under the ladder reads the whole wait back
  • Who hears about it is grouped by rung; buffer agents are people chips with seat use; next-transfer order is one segmented choice
  • One card with a section index and one save bar; if one of the two saves fails, the status line names it and keeps your edits
  • The status line shows today's transfers, median wait and expired count
