Your questions, answered

From the code, in plain words

Application form vs Application field set

Application formThe lead intake form. What a partner or agent fills in to create a lead, one per product (name, date of birth, state, tobacco, coverage wanted…). Used before anyone calls.
→
Application field setThe carrier's paper application. For each carrier and product: which fields are Not asked, Optional or Required on the case's Application step. Pre-submit checks refuse a case that misses a Required one.

One makes the lead; the other decides what the carrier needs later. Today they are not linked, only matched by field name. Proposed name: "Carrier application".

Quotation template vs Quote & QA rules

Quotation templateWhich inputs the Quote step asks, per carrier and product: face amount, tier, riders, age basis, how long a quote stays valid. It shapes the input. Proposed name: "Quote inputs".
→
Quote & QA rulesOne agency-wide set of checks on the result: price-per-$1,000 sanity band, block if not appointed, draft-day buffer, requirement ageing, welcome pack. Proposed name: "Quote & case checks".

Underwriting templates are the health questions on the Interview step; a knockout answer rules a carrier out. Proposed name: "Health questions".

Critique: what's wrong across Settings today

30 pages audited in code
1 · Five ways to saveSave-bar drafts, instant switches, a separate Save inside Business identity, publish-with-versions, and one dialog per row (Accounting, Retention). You never know if a change is kept.
2 · Tabs drawn four waysIn the header, in a bar inside the content (Carriers), as a page's own toolbar, and tabs inside tabs (Disclosures has 5, then 3 more in a document).
3 · The whole page scrollsOn all but five sections the title and tabs scroll away with the form.
4 · Same thing in many placesStages ×2, field maps ×4, time zone ×2, three phone numbers, licensed states ×3, alerts ×2, keys ×2, per-campaign rules ×2.
5 · Technical values shownWorkspace ID, key prefixes, URLs in monospace, pasted JSON, choices typed one per line, durations typed as "45 seconds".
6 · Problems are invisibleSave errors and "needs a database update" go to the bell, so the page just looks broken.
7 · Names that don't explainSix Sales process pages with near-identical names and no order (see above).
8 · Very long screensLead posting, Disclosures, Field sets and Calendar run 900–1,100 lines of controls.

The rules every page will follow

One way to save per kindSimple settings: one save bar at the foot of the card (or instant for a single switch, with Undo). Templates and forms: Draft → Publish with versions. Never two models on one page.
Title, tabs and actions on one lineSub-pages are the header underline bar. Nothing pushes the title down.
The card scrolls, not the pageHeader and tabs stay put; the form scrolls inside its card, with a section index on long forms.
A record opens as a right sheetA vendor, a template, a key or a person slides in from the right. Never a tab inside a tab, never a page swap with a back arrow.
A status line, not noticesUnder the title: "Saved · changed by Dana 2 days ago", "3 unsaved changes", or "Couldn't save, try again" in red. No notice cards.
Plain words, no internalsNo IDs, prefixes or JSON on screen. Technical details sit behind "Show technical details" with a Copy button.
One concept, one homeTime zone, phones, licensed states, field maps, alerts and keys each live in one place; other pages link there.
Colour rolesGreen acts, olive frames, gold highlights (people, new counts, one feature button), status chips mean status.
