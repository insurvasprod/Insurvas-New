Settings › Records › Settings history

Every change anyone makes in Settings, in one read-only list: who, when, where and what it was before. The same list shows inside My profile › History, filtered to your own changes. Today it is a wide audit table with technical values and From/Change columns, and each row jumps away to the setting without showing what changed.

1 · Values read like a databaseThe Change column shows keys and raw values ("price_band_max_cents: 1450 → 1500"). Nobody but an engineer reads that.
2 · Clicking a row leaves the pageA row links straight to the setting, so you lose your place and your filters to see one change.
3 · Filters are four dropdownsSection, person, type and date sit in a form row with no sign of which are on; there's no quick "last 7 days".
4 · Two-line rowsFrom and Change wrap, so 20 changes fill the screen and dates don't line up.
5 · Non-owners are confusedA producer sees an empty list or the page refuses, with no word that they only see their own changes.
