Three Agency pages that answer "who works here, what may they touch, and which carriers can they sell". Today Team is a stat strip over a paged table with two dialogs; Carriers offers three different ways to edit the same contracts (a setup tab, a carrier card dialog and a states tab) plus a hidden Field maps tab; the Browser extension page is four stacked cards. This wave-3 board shows one recommended design for each.

1 · Seats take a whole stripSeats, Pending invites, Buffer seats and Setter seats are four big tiles above the table, pushing the people down. They are a sentence's worth of facts.
2 · Leaving someone is a dead endManage only changes role or suspends. Nobody asks what happens to their 42 leads, callbacks and open cases, so work is orphaned when an agent leaves.
3 · One carrier, three editors"Set up %" edits the contract, the carrier card dialog edits it again, and "States" edits appointments a third way. The owner can't tell which one wins.
4 · Technical values up frontWriting numbers sit in the main grid and the field-map tab is hidden behind a flag, so the one thing that breaks commission imports is the hardest to find.
5 · The extension page is noiseAn install card, a carrier-sites table, a paged grants table with Revoke in every row and a facts card, all at the same weight. "Is it working, and who has it?" takes a scroll.
6 · Licensed states live in three placesThe person's row, the States tab and the carrier appointments. This board keeps one home: the person sheet, which reads appointments.

What changes vs today

no setting is removed; three editors become one sheet per record

Team & access

  • The four seat tiles become one status line
  • One-line people table with gold avatars; colour only in Status
  • Invite and Manage dialogs become right sheets
  • The person sheet adds Open work and a hand-over picker
  • Deactivate confirms what moves where; today only Suspend exists needs a migration
  • Licensed states have one home: the person sheet, read from appointments

Carriers

  • "Set up %", the carrier dialog and States become one table + one carrier sheet
  • The setup tab becomes the first-run page and the Add carrier dialog
  • States becomes a matrix, filterable by writer, cells edit appointments
  • The hidden Field maps tab moves into the sheet as Commission file
  • Writing numbers go behind "Show technical details"

Browser extension

  • Install state moves to the status line and a small side card
  • Carrier sites show monthly fills and whether each still works
  • Grants become "Who can use it", one line each, Revoke at the row's end
  • Revoke can be limited to one carrier needs a migration
  • The facts card becomes three numbered lines
