Settings › Sales process

These settings shape the agent's call, from the lead form to the moment the case is sent. Today they are six menu items with near-identical names: Underwriting templates, Quotation templates, Application field sets, Quote & QA rules, Disclosures, Application stages. The owner cannot tell which one changes which part of the call. This board renames each one after the step it controls, puts them in the order the agent meets them, and redraws each editor.

1 · Names say "template", not what they do"Underwriting template", "Quotation template" and "Field set" all mean "a list per carrier and product". Nothing says one is health questions, one is quote inputs and one is the carrier's paper form.
2 · No orderThe menu is alphabetical. The call runs Interview → Quote → Application → Disclosures → Review. The settings should follow that order.
3 · Same carrier × product grid three timesHealth questions, quote inputs and application fields each start with their own carrier picker. Each looks different, so the owner re-learns the screen every time.
4 · "Quote & QA rules" mixes two momentsThe price check runs on the Quote step. Appointment, draft date, requirement ageing and welcome pack run at Review and after submit. One page, two moments, no labels.
5 · Disclosures has tabs inside tabsFive page tabs, then three more inside a document. Records should open as a right sheet with sections, never a second row of tabs.
6 · Nothing says what needs youUnpublished drafts, carriers with no health questions and documents needing legal review are spread over six pages. Nothing adds them up in one place.
