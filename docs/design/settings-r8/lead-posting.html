Settings › Lead posting

Lead posting lets a lead vendor send leads straight into a campaign over the internet, the moment a person fills in their form. The agency owner has to connect each vendor, hand them an address and a key, agree which of the vendor's fields mean which of ours, and watch that leads keep arriving. Today the page can do all of that, but only someone technical could find their way: it is one long scroll of cards, the vendor gets nothing to read, and the only test is pasting raw lead data into a box.

1 · Too many cards, no centrePosting address, figure strip, key list, picked key with four tabs, then separate rejections and live-posts cards. The page scrolls; the owner cannot see "is every vendor fine?" in one look.
2 · The vendor gets nothingAfter the key is shown once, the owner must explain the address, the headers, the required fields and the replies by hand. There is no instructions page, PDF or email.
3 · Raw internals on showThe legacy address with the workspace id in monospace, key prefixes, and a box for pasting raw lead data. Plain-English owners stall here.
4 · Testing is a dry run only"Check a sample post" reads pasted text; nothing travels the real path. The vendor cannot send a test that is kept apart from real leads and billing.
5 · Field map stops at our basic fieldsTheir extra answers (coverage wanted, tobacco) cannot reach the product's own intake questions, and an unmatched field is only discovered when posts start failing.
6 · No health per vendor, no reply to the vendorAccepted share, speed and spend are spread across tabs; the vendor is never told afterwards whether a lead was accepted, contacted or sold.
