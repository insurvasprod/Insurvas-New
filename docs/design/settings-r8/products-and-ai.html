Settings › Products · AI assistant

Two Selling pages. Products decides which insurance products the agency sells; every product picker in the app (new lead, imports, partners, forms, quotes) reads this one answer. Today it is a single list card: a "Sell X" switch with Undo, a partners line, a carrier count and "Form ready" or "Set up its form", then a locked "Not in your plan" group. It doesn't say what else a product needs before an agent can actually quote it. AI assistant is today a read-only placeholder: a disabled switch, two disabled dropdowns and two lists of what would and would never be sent, because no AI provider is chosen yet. It explains a feature nobody can use instead of what the agency would get.

1 · "Sold" isn't "ready"Products only checks the intake form. A product can be on with no carrier offering it, no health questions and no quote inputs, so agents find out on a live call.
2 · Status hides in a sentence"Dana and SmartFinancial may send it · 4 carriers offer it · Form ready" is one grey line per row; you can't scan which products need work.
3 · The locked group is a dead end"Not in your plan" names products but not which plan adds them or what it costs; "See plans" drops you on Billing without the product in mind.
4 · AI page leads with a warningThe first thing is a warning card and a greyed switch. The owner never learns what the assistant would do on a call or why it's worth waiting for.
5 · Privacy is right, but buriedThe two "sent / never sent" lists are the strongest part of the page and sit under disabled dropdowns. They should frame the opt-in.
6 · No way to say "I want this"There's no opt-in, waitlist or "turn on when ready", so we learn nothing about demand and the owner has to remember to come back.

Example"$48.20 a month for $15,000. Full cover from day one."

Before Submit, checks the required disclosures were read and the recording consent was given, and names any gap.

Example"Replacement disclosure not read. Read it before submitting."
