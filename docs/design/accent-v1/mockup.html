A third brand colour

Green does the work: buttons, the chosen tab, the rail. Olive is the frame. The third colour is the highlight: what is new, what went up, the second line on a chart, people. It is never a button colour for everyday actions and never a status, so it can't be confused with "done", "needs action" or "stopped". Each option moves the one status colour it would clash with. The board shows the live light theme (White + olive lines) and the live dark theme.

① Numbers that went upThe change under a figure and the money highlight on the commission card.
② The second line on a chart"Submitted" next to green "Issued", and last period on Compare.
③ PeopleAvatars and initials, so a person reads differently from a status.
④ New and countsA "New" tag, the bell and rail counts (red stays for errors only).
⑤ One feature buttonAt most one per page, for a feature moment such as "Upgrade" or "Start Calling".

Gold (recommended)

Green, olive and gold is a classic money-and-trust palette: warm, premium, and the clearest contrast with green in both themes. "Needs action" moves from amber to burnt orange so it never reads as gold.

Coral

The true opposite of the green: energetic and friendly, the most "app-like". "Stopped" moves from red to a raspberry so the two never meet. Busier on a dense table page.

Indigo

A cool, techy contrast, calm on both themes. "In progress" moves from blue to teal-blue. The least warm; reads more "software" than "insurance".
