Admin › Catalog: one area with a rail

Catalog gets the same treatment as Billing. Hovering Catalog in the menu lists every page. Clicking it opens the Catalog workspace: what's waiting for review across plans, features and carriers. Inside, a rail on the left switches pages. Every one of today's 13 pages keeps its content and actions in a cleaner layout. The biggest change is new: eight pages are really one carrier split eight ways (rules, file layouts, status words, field maps, portal guidance, servicing, fact reports), so each carrier gets its own carrier record page with all of them in tabs. The cross-carrier pages stay in the rail for working across carriers.

Tile figures are from the live pages on Nov 17 where shown. Row names and the numbers in new features are illustrative.

1 · One carrier, eight pagesTo see everything about Americo you visit Carriers, Carrier rules, Layout maps, Status maps, Field maps, Portal guidance, Servicing instructions and Fact reports. Nothing links them.
2 · Facts you can't editA carrier's portal address, reference pattern and bank descriptor can only change by "fixing" an agency's fact report. Admins can't simply edit them.
3 · Counts that go nowhere"3 plans · 2 add-ons" on a feature, "4 templates · 2 partners" on a product: counts with no link to the rows behind them.
4 · No historyPlans publish versions but you can't see what changed between them, who is on which, or compare two plans. No page links to the audit log.
5 · Review queues hiddenStatus words waiting, fact reports waiting, field maps in review and carriers missing rules are each a tile on a different page. No single place says "this needs you".
6 · Old lookCapital-letter labels, notice cards, filters inside cards, dialogs for records, and several lists with no search (Field maps, Status maps, Servicing).
