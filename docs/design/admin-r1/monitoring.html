Admin › Monitoring: one area with a rail

Monitoring gets the same treatment as Billing and Catalog. Hovering Monitoring lists every page. Clicking it opens a security and activity workspace, and a rail on the left switches pages. Both of today's pages, Login activity and the Audit log, keep all their content and actions. Three things an admin has to hunt for today move in beside them: sign-in lockouts (now hidden under Platform › Advanced), staff sessions (signing a staff member out everywhere already works on the server but has no screen), and the jobs and services monitor (the Notion spec's "Error & job monitor", today only a dashboard tab).

Tile figures are from the live pages on Nov 17 where shown. Names, IPs and numbers in new features are illustrative.

1 · Two lists, no pictureLogin activity and the Audit log are long tables with tiles on top. Nothing shows a trend, a spike in failed sign-ins, or what changed since yesterday.
2 · Security tools scatteredLockouts and Unlock live in a panel under Platform › Advanced. Revoking a staff member's sessions has a route but no button. Jobs failing are on a dashboard tab.
3 · Details in pop-upsAn audit entry opens a centre dialog with raw before/after values; a sign-in attempt has no detail at all, so you can't see what else came from the same IP.
4 · Filters don't stickFilters live only on screen (except the audit log's target link), so a filtered view can't be shared, bookmarked or linked from elsewhere.
5 · Old lookCapital-letter tile labels, a filter panel that opens under the toolbar, and a "Retention · Kept indefinitely" tile that is a fact, not a figure.
