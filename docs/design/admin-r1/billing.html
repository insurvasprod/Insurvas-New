Admin › Billing: merge

Billing has 10 menu entries today. One of them, Billing workspace, is only four link cards, and the tab bar covers just 5 of the 10 pages. Several pages show the same thing in different words: MRR in three places, trials in two, "raise a credit note" in two, coupons and offers apart even though an offer is a campaign over a coupon. This board merges them without dropping a feature, a figure or a role rule. Every control on today's pages is listed under the screen that keeps it.

Figures in the tiles come from the live pages on Nov 17. Row names, coupon and offer names, the MRR movement amounts and dialog field labels are illustrative; the build uses today's data and today's dialog fields.

1 · A page of linksBilling workspace has no data and no actions, and repeats the tab bar. Subscriptions, Trials, Offers, Credits & limits and Setup aren't on that tab bar.
2 · Same data, three placesMRR is shown live on Subscriptions, from a snapshot on Revenue, and on the dashboard. Trials appear on Subscriptions and on Trials. Coupons and Offers are separate pages over the same discounts.
3 · Detail is a page swapThe invoice opens a new page with tabs. The subscription row expands in place but can't act, so you have to go to the customer record. Credit notes "raise" by sending you to another page.
4 · Old lookCapitalised labels, coloured big numbers, filters inside the card, and notice cards (pending, failed, stale) where the status line belongs.
5 · A broken linkThe dashboard's "past due" link opens Subscriptions with ?status=past_due, which the page ignores. The merged page reads every filter from the address.
6 · Roles must holdBilling ops can't open Credits & limits or Setup today; platform config sees only Credits & limits; support sees no billing. The merged page shows each role only its tabs.
