Admin › Billing: one area with a rail

Your direction: Billing works like Accounting, Compliance and Reports. Hovering Billing in the menu shows every page. Clicking it opens the Billing workspace, a simple overview of billing. Inside, a rail on the left switches pages. Every page keeps exactly the content and actions it has today, in a cleaner layout. Each page also gets what an admin is missing today, marked New, and two pages the Notion spec lists that were never built: Failed payments (§6.5 dunning queue) and Reconciliation (§12 provider reconciliation report). Each page's list of what stays and what's new sits under its screen.

Tile figures are live values from Nov 17. Row names, coupon and offer names, amounts in the sheets, and new-feature numbers are illustrative.

The frameTitle + tabs on one line, a status line under it, the step rail on the left (grouped, with counts), Payment provider at the rail's foot as Accounting has its settings. A record opens as a right sheet. Filters live in the address.
Roles holdBilling ops sees every step except Credits & limits and Payment provider. Platform config sees only Credits & limits. Support has no Billing. The rail and the hover menu show each role only its steps.
Better, not differentSame columns, filters, figures and actions as today. Capital-letter labels and coloured big numbers go; one chip per row carries the colour; notices move into the status line; the Trials and Subscriptions rows open sheets that can act.
New, and what it needsEach New item says what it needs: nothing (built from data we keep), a migration (a small table, paste script for you), or provider support (Whop must allow it).
