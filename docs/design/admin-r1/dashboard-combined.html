Admin › Dashboard: combined

Your pick: a combination. The first tab is all graphs: the business at a glance, every graph opening the tab that explains it. Then one tab per thing an admin has to watch. "Needs you" is the work queue from B. Money, Customers, Platform health and Security & staff each get A's figure cards, one-line tables and right-side sheets. Every graph reads data the platform already keeps: the daily metrics history (MRR and how it moved, collected, customers new and lost, trials, split by plan) and the sign-up funnel that Revenue already computes. Live values from Nov 17 where shown; trend shapes are illustrative until the nightly metrics run again.

Tabs on the title lineOverview · Needs you 9 · Money · Customers · Platform health · Security & staff. The tab is in the address, so a link opens it. Roles see only their tabs: support has no Money.
Overview: graphs only8 graph cards, each with its headline figure above its trend. Gold is only "last year" or a second series. No donuts (owner rule). Period switch: 30 days · 90 days · 12 months.
One status line for all tabs"3 checks failing · revenue figures are 57 days old" stays under the title on every tab, and its link opens Needs you.
Moved off the home page"Start here" goes to Help. "Admins by role" moves to Security & staff. "Arrange" and "Press 1–9" are gone.
