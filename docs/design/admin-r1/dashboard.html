Admin › Dashboard

The page every Insurvas admin opens first. Today it is a launchpad of tiles. The one thing that matters most, the 17 daily checks, is crammed into a single tile, and the headline figures come from a revenue snapshot that is 57 days old with nothing on the page saying so. The rebuild brings the admin home in line with the app side: a title line with views, a status line instead of coloured numbers, figure cards with sparklines, and one-line tables where each row opens what it counts. The goal comes from Notion "Basic Idea Super Admin Side" §9: it should show where the business is leaking.

Live figures from Nov 17: MRR, customers, collected, trials, past due and every check. The split by plan, the funnel, the trend lines and the customer names on the right are illustrative: none is computed today. The split by plan is a new query on existing tables; the funnel needs new counts.

1 · The checks are buriedDaily checks are the admin's real to-do list: 17 rows of long sentences inside one tile, cut short, with coloured words and a Refresh button. "Failing" and "Look" carry the colour, and so does the sentence.
2 · Stale numbers read as freshMRR shows $3,480.00 in big orange text, and only a footnote says "snapshot 57 days old". The nightly metrics job has been failing since Sep 21. Nothing in the header warns you.
3 · Tiles, not answersThere are 7 tiles of uneven height with large empty areas (Trials, Mismatched), plus "Press 1–9", "Arrange" and a second "Start here" tab of links. There is no trend, no split by plan and no sign-up funnel, which are the three things §9 asks for.
4 · Colour everywhereBig figures are drawn in orange or red, footnote dots are coloured, and check labels are coloured. The app-side rule is one text colour, colour only in one chip per row, and gold only for rises, people and new counts.
5 · Low-value tiles"Admins by role" (5 active, 50 deactivated) and "Start here" links take home-page space. They belong on Admin users and in Help.
6 · Not the app's frameThe page has no title line with tabs and no status line, and the launchpad grid is unlike every other page. The app side settled on title + tabs on one line, a quiet status line, figure cards, one-line tables and right-side sheets.

Pick one

Nothing is built until you choose. After the dashboard, the same pattern goes to every admin page, group by group.
A · Today + views (recommended)Same frame as the app's dashboard. The checks get the most space, figures sit above them, and Money, Customers and Platform health are one tab away.
  • Status line says what's broken, and stale figures go grey with their age
  • 5 figure cards with sparklines, gold only for rises
  • "Needs you" is a one-line table, and each row opens a sheet with the rows it counts plus the fix
  • Customers tab: the sign-up funnel from §9 needs new counts
  • Drops "Start here", "Arrange", "Press 1–9" and "Admins by role"
B · Work queueBest if admins treat the home page as a to-do list and little else. Each check gets a full detail pane with history and its action.
  • Figures squeezed to one line
  • Check history needs a migration
  • No trend or split by plan on the home page
C · Business overviewNotion §9 as drawn: best for the owner reading the business, weakest for daily operations.
  • 12-month MRR chart with last year in gold
  • Checks hidden behind a status-line link
  • Funnel needs new counts
