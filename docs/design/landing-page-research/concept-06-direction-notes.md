# Concept 06 — direction notes

Research artifact, 3 November 2026. This concept starts a fresh visual direction; it is not a revision of the rejected landing-page styling. No production files or product behavior were changed.

## Creative idea: the work behind the number

Treat the homepage like a confident product publication: restrained typography, hard rules, quiet green accents, and actual Insurvas screens presented as evidence. The creative subject is the gap between buying a lead and understanding what happened to it. Keep the data visible when it is incomplete. The page should feel like a serious insurance operation, not an imagined dashboard or a futuristic animation demo.

## Why this direction fits

- The manager's recording asks for section-by-section storytelling, a story understandable to a newcomer, and inspiration assembled from different sites without copying a whole site. See [manager-video-notes.md](manager-video-notes.md); transcript wording remains automatic and uncertain.
- The CRM review found real moments more persuasive than a linear success story: an import whose checks were still pending, a manually entered deal with no campaign attribution, and a separate policy-to-statement match. See [workflow-story-evidence-2026-11-03.md](workflow-story-evidence-2026-11-03.md).
- [SaaSUI](https://www.saasui.design/) is useful as a library of shipped product patterns, not as a page template. [Lapa Ninja's Mintlify recording](https://www.lapa.ninja/post/mintlify-2/) is useful for observing pacing and the relationship between product and explanation. The supplied Behance moodboard and Saaspo were not accessible in this text-only research pass. PowerHours and AgentCompass are documented in [reference-analysis-2026-11-03.md](reference-analysis-2026-11-03.md).

## What the visitor can do

1. Read the saved homepage headline and existing explanation over a genuine product screen.
2. Select a sample call result. The next-step explanation changes among callback, no answer, and submitted; it states clearly that this local interaction does not update Insurvas.
3. Browse the three genuine test-workspace screenshots: import, deal details, and a statement line. The captions keep those records separate and say exactly what was and was not verified.
4. Follow the live pricing link, where the current catalog controls plan details.

## Evidence and guardrails

- The import screenshot says three rows are ready while the checks are pending. It must not be described as a completed import.
- The deal screenshot shows no campaign or partner. It must not be used as proof of source attribution.
- The statement screenshot shows one fictional line accepted and posted to the test ledger. The expected commission is unavailable because the sample has no configured carrier rate. This is a policy-to-statement match, not the same deal or import.
- The call-outcome control is illustrative, based on available CRM outcomes. It does not claim that the pictured deal had that call result.
- The images are actual captures from the authorized test workspace; sample records are fictional. The concept does not use generated agents, invented testimonials, made-up performance figures, or an asserted end-to-end conversion.
- Existing homepage copy is retained; this direction changes only its presentation and adds descriptive sample captions.

## Motion and interaction

Motion is tied to a visitor action: selecting a call outcome changes the described next step; selecting a CRM screenshot changes the explanation and source image. Scroll reveal only stages supporting sections, and the reduced-motion rule keeps all content visible without movement. Nothing advances automatically or hides the explanatory copy behind an animation.

## Review limit

Concept 06 is a standalone HTML mockup. Its design and interactions have not been browser-verified. The provided CRM screenshots are real, but some were captured during test workflows and are illustrative rather than production marketing proof.
