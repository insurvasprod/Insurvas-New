2 · The page pattern

One pattern on every page of all three planes
1Title line

Trail, title, (i), the sub-page bar, one primary button on the right.

2Sub-page bar

Every sibling page is a tab. ≤ 6 tabs; extra ones in More ▾. No step rail beside it.

3List

One-line table, ≤ 6 columns, one chip, a verb › at the end. Filters as chips over the table.

4Record page

Same title line: name, status line, tabs (Overview · … · History), the next step as the primary button.

5Wizard

Anything with more than 5 fields or 2 decisions: one question per step, progress bar, More options, Back always works, answers kept.

6Overlays

Dialog: short edit. Right sheet: a quick create or a short wizard (≤ 4 steps). Full page: long wizard (application). Toast: confirm. Never a side panel for reading.

3A · Homes: Do next

The next 3–5 jobs as big buttons, figures and charts under them

3B · Homes: Your day

A timeline of today's jobs in time order, figures in a side column
