# Accounting in Insurvas: how each page works

*6 November 2026. Shared copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/1UhDfEeJtjTmxeQXhqbmA9*

This is where you see your agency's money in one place. Insurvas keeps track of it, but it never sends or moves money for you. You still pay people the way you always have.

## Profit & loss

- Did we make money this month, and what did each sale really cost us?
- Choose the month → see what came in and what went out → see what each policy cost you, and what it cost once you count the ones that fell off → click any number to see where it came from

## Bank deposits

- Did the money the carriers said they paid actually land in the bank?
- Drop in the carrier's commission file and your bank statement → Insurvas links what the carrier said it paid to what landed, from that day to a few days after → then checks every policy against what you were promised → Results shows each payment as All good, Missing money or Bad math → open one to link the deposit, accept it, or draft a dispute letter

## Vendor bills

- The bills from your lead vendors, checked before you pay them.
- Type in the bill → Insurvas compares it with the leads you really got → you approve it → mark it paid, or push back if it's wrong → refunds and returns live in their own tab

## Expenses

- Where the agency's money goes, in three buckets: buying leads, monthly subscriptions, and daily & random.
- Add an expense: what it is, how much, which bucket, and whether it comes back every month → a monthly bill comes back each month as a to-do in the bell → you click OK and it's booked and marked paid → the Scoreboard shows what each month cost, in total and per bucket, with lead invoices from Vendor bills counted under Leads

## Partner payouts

- What you owe each call centre or partner, worked out from the rulebook you set for them, and never paid twice.
- Set their rulebook (Model A: a flat fee once a transfer's application is submitted, or Model B: a share of the commission when the policy is issued) → pick weekly, every 15 days or monthly, who carries chargebacks, and who gets paid → every client they send goes into the ledger → press Run payouts and each partner whose period has ended gets an invoice → publish it and they see it, with a PDF, in their own login → mark it paid and every item on it is stamped PAID → a chargeback comes off their next invoice

## Producer payouts (Team payouts)

- Paying your own team each month, by each person's pay plan: solo or sidekick (a flat fee per saved deal), salary + bonus, or a big split.
- Set each person's pay plan → pick the month and the people → look at the draft, nothing is saved yet → for anyone below zero, carry it, take a share this month, or let it go → say how you paid each person (bank transfer, Zelle, check) → confirm and the month is stamped paid, with a private statement for each person

## Producer splits

- How much of each sale each agent keeps, and where they stand right now.
- Set each agent's share → their sales and chargebacks add up on their own → if someone ends up owing money, you decide to carry it, take it back slowly, or let it go → agents see only their own balance

## Commission splits

- For the times two agents share one policy.
- Pick the policy and the two agents → agree on the split (it has to add up to 100%) → the money divides the same way → if it charges back, both take the hit in the same share

## Tax summaries

- Your numbers ready for tax time. It doesn't file anything for you.
- Pick the year → see who you paid enough to need a 1099 → add their details and W-9 → see the year sorted the way your accountant likes → download it and send it to them

## Accounting settings

- Your rules for all of the above, like agent splits and when the year starts. You'll find it under Settings.
