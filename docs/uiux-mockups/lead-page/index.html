Today, and what's wrong with it

From your screenshot of Hayden Thomas. Each point names the house rule it breaks; every direction fixes all of them.

#What the page doesWhy it's wrong
1No buttons on the title line. The actions are a stack of full-width buttons in a side card called “Actions”.One primary button on the title line, with ⋯ last (owner rule). Today you hunt for what to do next.
2A big identity card with an empty “— verified” ring, and the phone as raw digits (2052337210).The ring says nothing until verification starts; it takes the best spot on the page. Numbers aren't formatted.
3Seven separate fact boxes (Lead, Submitted, Owner, Verification, Quoted, Attempts, Next action).A wall of boxes, not a strip. Two say nothing (Next action “—”, Lead ID). None links anywhere.
4Seven tabs inside a card, under the boxes.The sub-page bar belongs on the title line (§8b). Attempts, Callbacks and Timeline are three views of one history.
5“A callback is booked. When it comes due the lead is back in the queue…” and “Insurvas has not checked this lead… Policy matching is not included yet”.Explanation paragraphs on the page (no notice cards, §3).
6A Stage dropdown in the side card that changes the stage without an outcome.Stage is the lead's headline fact; it belongs by the name, and a move should read like the board.
7“Not provided” six times in the form.Empty fields shout as loud as real ones.
8Content held to a centre column with dark bands either side.Fluid full width (owner rule, 6 Nov).
9“Can they finish on this call?” as six big Yes / No / Not asked rows mid-form.It's the agent's checklist for the next step, not part of what was submitted.

Record, tidied

today's layout, every rule applied — the least change

The right column keeps only real content: what happens next, source and cost, and the existing-customer check as one line.

B

Call cockpit

three columns, like Close and HubSpot records: person · activity · next step

Built for the agent on the phone. Left is the person: phone with Call and Text, their local time, contact and coverage facts, the source. Centre is one activity stream: calls, callbacks, notes, partner messages, stage moves and screening, newest first, with the note box on top. Right is the next step: the stage and how long the lead has been there, the next callback, the six “can they finish?” questions as a short checklist, then the one primary button. The tabs shrink to three because the stream holds the rest.
