Four Setup steps of Carrier sync: Carrier terms, Card moves, Agent codes, Files in. For each: a short critique of today's screen, three directions for its main page with one marked, then every sub-page, pop-up, confirm, empty state, error line, toast and the phone view in the marked direction. The edit map closes the file. Copy is taken from the code (components/app/book/setup/*, carrier-file-upload, carrier-file-doors, carrier-bucket, book-import); the data is a demo agency.

  • Rules applied on every board: the title alone on its line (no facts line, nothing beside it); tabs and buttons on the title line over one hairline; the Carrier sync rail on the left; tables fill to the window foot with the pager at the foot; every figure and name links to its filtered page; no notice cards (pending and read-only states become an empty state or a disabled button with its reason); text 12 / 14 / 16 only.
