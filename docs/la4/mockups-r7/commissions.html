Tables, one line (owner 2026-11-11)

Owner: two or three lines a row and many font colours, and people don't know where to focus. Every table in Commissions takes the Vendor bills B look: a row is one line, the text is one colour, colour lives only in one chip. The A boards below are drawn this way (the sub-lines and coloured amounts in their source are switched off by this page's style).

RULESfor every table, list and record page
  1. One line per row. No grey line under a name. What it said gets its own column when a reader chooses rows by it, else it waits on the record page.
  2. One text colour. First column semibold, everything else regular in the body colour; muted only for "—" and dates. Amounts tabular, never green, amber or red, never bold.
  3. Colour only in a chip. One status chip a row (dot + a few words). Its tone is the meaning: waiting, wrong, done. A problem amount is said in the chip ("$270.00 short"), not by tinting the number.
  4. The next step is a short verb the same weight in every row (Review ›, Send letter ›, Pay ›); the whole row opens the record.
  5. Five or six columns. 14px cells, 12px headers, 44px rows, tables fill the window.
