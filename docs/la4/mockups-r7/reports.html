Tables, one line (owner 2026-11-11)

Owner: two or three lines a row and many font colours, and people don’t know where to focus. Every Reports table takes the look Commissions, Accounting, Retention, Book of business and Carrier sync already have: a row is one line, the text is one colour, colour lives only in one chip.

RULESfor every table view, list and record page in Reports
  1. One line per row. No grey line under a name. What it said gets its own column when a reader chooses rows by it, else it waits on the record page or a tooltip.
  2. One text colour. First column semibold, everything else regular in the body colour; muted only for “—” and dates. Figures tabular and plain: never green, amber or red, never bold.
  3. Colour only in a chip. One status chip a row. A problem figure is said in the chip (“150 logged without a dial”, “$1,132.70 at risk”), not by tinting the number. A small sample is a chip (“Under 5 cases”), not a grey number.
  4. The next step is a short verb plus › (Review ›, Close out ›), only on rows that wait on someone; the whole row opens the record.
  5. Five or six columns. A matrix that grows a column per carrier (Decline reasons, Requirements) turns long: one row per reason, the carrier is a filter and a chart. A pair in one cell (“70 / 23”, “87 % ( 271 / 312 )”) becomes one figure; the other is in the tooltip or its own column.
