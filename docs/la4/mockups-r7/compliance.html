Tables, one line (owner 2026-11-11)

Owner: two or three lines a row and many font colours, and people don't know where to focus. Every Compliance table takes the Vendor bills B look: a row is one line, the text is one colour, colour lives only in one chip.

RULESfor every table, list and record page
  1. One line per row. No grey line under a name. What it said gets its own column when a reader chooses rows by it, else it waits on the record page or a tooltip.
  2. One text colour. First column semibold, everything else regular in the body colour; muted only for "—" and dates. Counts tabular, never green, amber or red, never bold.
  3. Colour only in a chip. One status chip a row. Its tone is the meaning: waiting, wrong, done. "Overdue by 9 days" is said in the chip, not by tinting the date.
  4. The next step is a short verb the same weight in every row (Respond ›, Verify ›, Add proof ›, Open ›); the whole row opens the record.
  5. Five or six columns. 14px cells, 12px headers, 44px rows, tables fill the window. A raw-file table (a number's timeline, the appointment grid's export) may keep the columns it needs, one line a row.
