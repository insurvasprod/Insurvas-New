16Edit map · where every decision and edit happens
  • Review page Every Needs you decision (all 12 kinds) and every pay issue. Opened from: a file's "Review N", a Files row's button, a Needs you line, a Pay issues line, the bell, and a row's "Review" link. One question, numbered answers, auto-next, bulk from the queue.
  • Dialog Bulk answers (Mark 25 as mine / Not ours), Mark a rule verified, Replace with a corrected file, leave with unsaved changes.
  • In the row A carrier rule (window, grace, redraft, source, from, note), a money or file fact on the carrier page, a rate on the rate card.
  • Popover Client search on "Someone in Insurvas", sale search on "Link to a sale".
  • Page A file, a row, a policy (tabs, with Money), a carrier's rules, Change client, a statement, a ledger line.
  • Removed Deciding inside a table row (file rows, Needs you list), the one-item More menu on a row, per-row Open buttons, Setup tabs cut off at the edge.
  • Bell Stop reasons, unfinished steps, unreadable sources: never on the page.
