CRITIQUEwhat the page does today, and why it reads as a different product
  1. A label wall, not a summary. Overview is nine "label over sentence" pairs in two thin columns; the answer to "is this policy OK?" (at risk, 191 days behind, critical) is buried in sentences. Every other redesigned page opens with the mockups' strip of figures.
  2. Two reds and an orange in the title. "7 payments behind", "Provisional" and "Check the client" compete beside the name; the facts line is cut off before the agent.
  3. A collapsible explainer. "Where each value comes from" is a closed <details> under the card; the owner's rule is no collapsible explainers. Provenance belongs on each value.
  4. Half the window is empty. The page doesn't fill the height; the carrier's raw row (the most useful evidence) is hidden.
  5. The wrong primary. "Open in Save Queue" goes to the radar the call of 2026-11-05 retired; More is a text button, not ⋯ last.
  6. Each tab is drawn differently. Client is a fact grid, Pipeline a sentence and a button over a table, Money three stacked cards plus notices on the page (no notices: they go to the bell).
