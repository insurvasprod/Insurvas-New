CRITIQUEwhat the page does today, and what makes it hard
  1. The title line carries everything. "Issued → Issued · Live" then "Issued · Every carrier · version 4 · live since 2026-11-04" in large type: five facts, a raw ISO date, and the event twice (it is already the name).
  2. No "what now". Three equal buttons (Duplicate, Turn off, Back to forecast) and nothing says what this page wants from you. For a Forecast rule the job is "check the would-moves, then switch to live"; for a Live rule, "nothing, unless it moved a card wrong". The page shows neither.
  3. 27 chips. "Only when it is in" lays out every stage of every pipeline as chips in a scrolling box: the answer ("27 of 31 stages") is buried in the control.
  4. Save hides at the foot, under the note card, cut off at 1440 × 900. Preview on today's data and the Preview tab do the same thing twice.
  5. Forecast and Preview are two tabs for one question ("what does this rule do?"): one looks back 7 days, the other at today.
  6. Notices on the page (carriers with no grace, lines marked wrong) — the owner's rule is: no notices; say it on the thing it is about.
